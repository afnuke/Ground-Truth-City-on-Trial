# The Southeast — Where It Stands

**South Carolina: 41 cities scoped, 37 cases built.** Every South Carolina city on the docket whose audited books are published is in the app — ten cases live in the current build, and the rest are in the final South Carolina build now in assembly (November 5, 2026). Every figure in every case is traced to the city's own audited financial report and verified figure by figure before it ships.

Four cities are waiting on the cities themselves:

- **Aiken** — its audit was never published. A records request for it is on file.
- **Rock Hill** — the audit is published, but locked behind a website that refuses automated access.
- **North Augusta** — its audits sit inside a document system that doesn't answer.
- **Cayce** — its newest audit exists only inside a protected meeting packet.

When those records exist, those cases open. Until then, the chairs stay empty and labeled — we don't fill them with estimates.

**The research for the rest of the Southeast is already pulled:**

- North Carolina — 89 cities scoped, audits hunted
- Georgia — 98 cities scoped, audits hunted
- Florida — 168 cities scoped, audits hunted

That's **355 more cities** whose books have already been found and read.

**Why it waits:** finishing them — verifying every figure the same way, city by city, and building the cases — is a funding question, not a knowledge question. At current costs, the whole remaining Southeast is **about a $50 project, total**. Small money. It just isn't money we have to spare right now, so the Southeast waits until it's funded.

If you'd like to sponsor a state — or the whole Southeast — reach out through GitHub ([@afnuke](https://github.com/afnuke)). Sponsorship is credited here, in the open, like everything else in this project.

We present the record. We don't render a verdict.
