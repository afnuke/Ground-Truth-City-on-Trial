# The records requests — the record

**Ground Truth: City on Trial** is a data-analysis project built by Bryan Lee (Afnuke), in AI collaboration. It presents South Carolina cities' audited financial statements as cited exhibits — the record, with sources, and no verdict rendered.

City employee compensation is a public record in South Carolina (S.C. Code §30-4-40(a)(6)), but not every city publishes it. So on **November 4, 2026**, we sent one identical Freedom of Information Act request — for that compensation record — to the cities in our study of 41 South Carolina municipalities:

- **37 cities** accepted the request electronically the same day, by email, web form, or public-records portal.
- **4 cities** already publish the record (Columbia and James Island by name; Beaufort and Moncks Corner by position), so no request was needed.
- **1 city — Anderson — could not be filed with electronically at all.** The City of Anderson publishes no email address, no web form, and no portal for records requests, and its downloadable request form states no destination for submission. Every other city in the study provides an electronic route; Anderson is the only one that requires mail or in-person delivery. Its published fee schedule ($20.00 per hour for staff time; $0.50 per page for copies) carries the highest per-page copy rate in the study.

That finding is part of this project's record, and it is documented in Anderson's case file in this repository. On the same day, it was also brought to the attention of the Governor of South Carolina and the South Carolina Attorney General, through each office's official contact form — a matter of record, stated here so the repository says plainly what was done and why:

A resident who wants a record the law makes public should be able to ask for it. Where a city publishes no way to ask electronically, we note it — the same way we note every figure in every case: from the record, with the source, and without a verdict.

*Determinations on the 37 requests are due under S.C. Code §30-4-30(C) by November 18–19, 2026. Each city's response — including any fee quoted to produce the record — will be recorded in its case file as it is received.*
