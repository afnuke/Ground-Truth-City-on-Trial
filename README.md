# Ground Truth: City on Trial — Volume Two

**▶ [The Volume Two pitch (62 seconds)](city-on-trial-pitch-v2-cover.mp4)** — the case for Volume Two, in one minute.

Volume One put companies and institutions on the stand — with their own filings. Volume Two puts the **cities** there.

Every city publishes an audited Annual Comprehensive Financial Report each year. Hundreds of pages. Almost nobody reads it. **City on Trial reads it — and hands you the exhibits.**

## The case file

Each city is a case. Its own audited book is the evidence, presented as six exhibits:

- **Exhibit A — Money In, Money Out.** The whole budget, per resident.
- **Exhibit B — The Debt.** What the city owes, per household.
- **Exhibit C — The Tax Dollar.** Where a tax dollar actually lands, cent by cent. (In Greenville's book, 46¢ of every General Fund dollar goes to public safety.)
- **Exhibit D — The Payroll.** The top public salaries — public record.
- **Exhibit E — The Reserve.** The rainy-day fund: saved, or spent down.
- **Exhibit F — The Growth.** This year's book against five years ago.

The app presents the record. It does not render a verdict — that's the jury's job, and the jury is you.

## The data law

- **Audited filings only.** Every figure comes from the city's own ACFR, with the source and fiscal year on the figure itself — the same standard Volume One holds companies to with their 10-Ks.
- **Denominators from the U.S. Census** (ACS), never estimates dressed up as facts.
- **Honest nulls.** An exhibit a city doesn't publish is shown as *not published* — never filled in with estimates, aggregator data, or guesses. An empty chair in the courtroom is evidence too.
